Tachora: download your tachograph card and check driving and rest times

Download card and tachograph (USB and Bluetooth), check driving and rest times.

Android · Google Play

Tachora is the app for truck drivers who want to understand their own tachograph data without waiting for the office.

Plug a USB card reader into your phone, insert the driver card and Tachora downloads it into a .DDD file. With compatible smart tachographs you can also download over Bluetooth.

Main features

  • Download the card with a compatible USB reader (Gen2 too) and the tachograph over Bluetooth, where the tachograph allows it.
  • A 24-hour chart for every day: driving, work, availability and rest.
  • Totals per day, week and two weeks; places, vehicles, events and inspections.
  • Check of driving and rest times under Regulation (EC) 561/2006 as amended by 2020/1054.
  • For every possible infringement, an explanation and an indicative fine estimate by country.
  • How much you can still drive today, before the break, this week and over two weeks.
  • Working time, overtime, night and holiday work under the collective agreement you choose.
  • Verification of the files' digital signatures, a map of positions, a reminder to download every 28 days.
  • PDF, CSV and Excel reports; an archive on the phone and an optional copy on your own Google Drive.

Trial and subscription

Tachora is free for 10 days from installation. After the trial, to keep using all the features, there is the Tachora Pro subscription, monthly or yearly, managed from Google Play. Even when the trial is over you can always save your files.

Your data stays yours

The archive is on your phone. Tachora has no server and does not send your data to us. All the details are in the privacy policy.
