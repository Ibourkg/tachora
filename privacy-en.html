Tachora: privacy policy

Last updated: 01/11/2026 (1 November 2026)

Tachora is an Android app for goods vehicle drivers: it reads the data of the driver card and of the tachograph, analyses it and produces reports. This page explains which data it processes and where that data goes.

Which data it processes

  • Tachograph data: the files downloaded from the driver card or from the tachograph (activities, places, vehicles, events, recorded positions), including the card's identification data (name, card number, dates).
  • Data you enter: settings (contract, language, country of the inspection) and places you add by hand on the map.
  • Google account: only if you turn on backup, the address of the account you sign in with, in order to save the copy to your Google Drive.

Where the data is kept

  • All data stays on your phone. Tachora has no server and receives none of your data: the only external connections are those described below (Google Drive if you turn it on, Google Play for the subscription, the map, the time check).
  • No advertising, no usage statistics, no tracking, no sale or transfer of data.

Google Drive (optional)

  • If you turn on backup, Tachora saves a copy of the downloaded files and of the places added by hand to the "Tachora" folder of your own Google Drive, using your account.
  • Tachora uses only the drive.file permission: it can see and manage only the files it creates itself, not the rest of your Drive.
  • Data received from Google APIs is used only for the backup and restore you request; it is not transferred to others, used for advertising or read by people. Its use complies with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
  • You can turn backup off at any time from the app and revoke access at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions. Files already saved stay in your Drive until you delete them.

Map

The map downloads the map images from OpenStreetMap. As with any website, the OpenStreetMap server sees the phone's IP address and the area requested; no card data is sent.

Free trial and subscription

  • Tachora is free for 10 days from installation. To work out when the trial ends, the app uses the installation date recorded by Android on your phone, which is not sent to anyone.
  • To check that the phone's clock is correct, when a network is available the app makes a request with no data to www.googleapis.com and reads only the time of the response.
  • The Tachora Pro subscription is bought and managed through Google Play: Google handles the payment under its privacy policy. Tachora does not see your payment details: it only receives from Google Play whether the subscription is active, and remembers it on the phone.
  • You can cancel the subscription at any time from Google Play (Payments and subscriptions). Even when the trial is over or without a subscription you can always save your files.

Sharing

Files leave the phone only when you decide so (for example by saving a report to the Downloads folder).

Deletion

You can delete each download from the app's Archive. Uninstalling the app deletes all data on the phone. You delete the copy on Drive from your own Drive.

App permissions

Bluetooth and location (only to find the tachograph, on phones that require it; the phone's location is not recorded), USB (card reader), notifications (download reminder), Internet (Drive, map, time check), Google Play billing (subscription).
